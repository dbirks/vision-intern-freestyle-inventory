# Public reference imagery: Freestyle ingredient cartridges

Research date: 2026-11-26. Scope: public sources only; no private cabinet photos consulted. The catalog names below are the requested class names, not verified machine identifiers. These are historical examples, and packaging, machine model, country and installation can change the visual presentation.

**Important distinction:** the 2011 cabinet photograph shows branded panels in black retaining doors, seen head-on. It does not establish that all the visible printing is on the removable package itself. The maps show intended slot/brand graphics and are not photographs of cartridge ends. Neither should be used as ground truth for an unseen box face.

## Part 1 versus Part 2

The UK map explicitly distinguishes each of Coca-Cola, Coca-Cola zero, Diet Coke and Dr Pepper using **CART 1** and **CART 2** in adjacent diagram positions. These are diagram labels, not proof of a numeral printed on an outward-facing physical end. A [public court complaint](https://docketnexus.com/document/467363481/) describes Coca-Cola, Coca-Cola Zero Sugar and Diet Coke double cartridges as two attached bags with product labels identifying P1/Part 1 and P2/Part 2; this is an allegation and label transcription, not independent photographic verification of the outward face. The [Zero Sugar listing](https://www.ebay.com/itm/407281499362) advertises a 2 × 23 fl oz double cartridge, consistent with equal stated nominal contents for that example. Coca-Cola's [cartridge management guide](https://crewconnect.coca-cola.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/CCFS-CartridgeManagement-Quick-Tip-1.pdf) says double cartridges are glued together and shows single and double trays.

**Unresolved:** no reliable, face-on comparison here establishes whether each brand's physical P1 and P2 ends bear a printed 1/2, use different colored bands, or differ in dimensions. Equal nominal volume does not prove equal external size. Do not infer a universal color or size rule from a slot map, or transfer one brand's arrangement to another.

## Gaps and interpretation

No verified public close-up in this collection identifies the physical outward end of either part for all four requested paired brands, nor physical ends for AA, CC or NNS. BB is retained solely as the user's catalog alias, not independently established. The cabinet photo and maps are useful for visual context and candidate cues, not current universal templates or proof of underlying packaging. More properly licensed, clear, dated close-ups of individual labeled cartridges would be needed to resolve those gaps. The manifest identifies the one downloaded direct image; documents, marketplace and video are linked rather than converted or downloaded.
