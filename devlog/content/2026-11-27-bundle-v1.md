Bundle `rfdetr-m-1280-v1` came out of validation-only tuning:

- **Locate:** RF-DETR Medium on 1280 px tiles, then the new duplicate suppression (containment +
  same-shelf-cell check).
- **Shelves:** capped at 2. The 2-high NNS stack now counts as a tier, not a third shelf.
- **Identify (hybrid):** Inkling 2 Mini names the *brand family* from colour and logo. Embedding
  nearest-neighbour with a **class-balanced** gallery (at most 10 crops per class) only refines
  within that family. Low confidence → `unknown`.
- **Pair rule:** in report mode.

**Best frame (validation), v0 → v1:**
- v0: NNS 8, Hi-C 4, Fanta 3, Vanilla 2, unknown 9.
- v1: **NNS 7**, AA 1, Fanta 2, CC 1, Sprite 1, Orange 1, Vanilla 1, Lemon 2, unknown 7.

The 7 NNS is what's physically there (a 3-stack plus 4). On another frame, NNS dropped from 22
(absurd) to 5. Still not scored: these are my visual impressions, not ground truth.

**Known gaps:**
- the bottom shelf (the Coke family) is mostly `unknown`. The auto-labelled gallery has almost
  no confident Part 1/Part 2 examples, so it can't learn them. Your quiz answers would fix that
  directly.
- the class-agnostic detector sometimes emits a phantom second class channel, which blocks
  lowering its score threshold. Codex is digging into RF-DETR's head setup to handle it
  properly.

**Cost:** about 15–20 s per photo on CPU, plus about 3 s per batch of Inkling calls for
identity.
It needs the Collective API key at inference time.
